# Z1RR RaceTime acceptable use and moderation

Last updated: August 23, 2026

## Use the service fairly

Z1RR RaceTime exists for organized Zelda 1 Randomizer competition. Protect
race integrity, follow the announced rules for each event, and treat racers,
volunteers, commentators, and spectators with respect.

The following cheating and abuse are prohibited.
You may not use the service to:

- cheat, falsify results, evade tournament restrictions, coordinate prohibited
  assistance, or interfere with an integrity review;
- engage in harassment, threats, hateful conduct, targeted abuse,
  impersonation, doxxing, or unwanted sexual content;
- disrupt rooms, spam chat or race creation, probe other accounts, evade
  moderation, or attempt unauthorized access;
- publish malware, unlawful material, private credentials, or content you do
  not have the right to share; or
- use automated access that is not an approved bot or documented API client,
  or place unreasonable load on the service.

## Event rules and moderation

Event-specific rules may add requirements for flags, scheduling, streaming,
communication, or evidence. Where event rules conflict with ordinary
pickup-race expectations, the published event rules control for that event.

Depending on context and severity, moderators may warn a user, remove content,
restrict chat or room participation, invalidate a result, suspend an account,
or ban access. Urgent protective action may be taken before a full review.
Material competitive-integrity matters are handled under the applicable event
process, with relevant audit records preserved.

## Report a problem

Use the Z1Rracing Discord's designated moderation or event channel. Include
the race link, approximate time, and a concise description; send sensitive
evidence privately. Do not publish credentials or personal information in a
public report.
