# Z1RR RaceTime account deletion

Last updated: August 23, 2026

## Delete through account settings

Sign in, open Account settings, and choose Delete account. Deletion is blocked
while you are participating in an active race so the room can finish
consistently. Leave or finish the race, then try again.

## What happens

When deletion completes:

- the live account, Discord external identity, profile, and optional Twitch
  connection are deleted;
- entrant comments are removed;
- system messages that identified the account are anonymized where supported;
  and
- historical race results, timing, and rankings may remain in anonymized form
  so completed race records stay coherent.

The deleted account cannot be restored through the site. A later sign-in
creates a new account and does not reclaim the deleted account's private data
or ownership automatically.

## Backups

Deleted records may remain in encrypted backups until the applicable retention
point expires. Recovery points roll through:

- 14 days of daily-equivalent backups;
- three months of weekly backups; and
- one year of monthly backups.

Backup copies are used only for disaster recovery; they are not searched or
edited to process an ordinary deletion.

## Get help

If you cannot sign in or deletion fails, use the Z1Rracing Discord contact
route. Do not post your Discord token, session cookie, OAuth code, or other
credential. An operator may ask for enough non-secret information to identify
the account and verify the request.
