# Z1RR RaceTime privacy notice

Last updated: August 23, 2026

## Who operates this service

Z1RR RaceTime is operated by Z1Rracing for organized Zelda 1 Randomizer
racing. It is an independent deployment of the open-source Racetime project.

## Data we collect

- **Account identity:** your Discord user ID, the Racetime display name you
  choose, account timestamps, and preferences. Discord profile data returned
  during sign-in is used only to complete authentication. The Discord
  authorization code and access token are transient and are not stored.
- **Optional profile and connection data:** information you add to your
  profile, and your Twitch ID, login, display name, and connection data if you
  choose to link Twitch.
- **Race activity:** race entries, timing, results, comments, chat, moderation
  actions, rankings, and related room history needed to operate and preserve
  race records.
- **Security and operations:** IP address and user-agent data associated with
  account actions, plus rotated application and proxy logs used for security,
  abuse prevention, troubleshooting, and moderation.

## How we use and share data

We use this data to authenticate accounts, run races and leaderboards, enforce
race integrity and community rules, secure the service, and recover from
failures. Public profile and race information is visible to other users as
part of normal Racetime operation.

Data is processed by Discord or Twitch when you use those integrations and by
infrastructure providers that host the service and its encrypted backups.
Personal data is not sold, and it is not used for third-party advertising
profiles.

## Cookies and tokens

The site uses necessary session and security cookies. OAuth access and refresh
tokens issued to approved clients are used to provide the integrations you
authorize. Do not share session or OAuth credentials.

## Retention and deletion

Security and operational logs are rotated and are not kept indefinitely.
Moderation and audit records may remain with the race or account action they
document. When you delete your account, the live account and connected Discord
identity are removed; historical race records are anonymized where they must
remain coherent.

Production database and media recovery points are retained on a rolling
schedule:

- daily-equivalent points for 14 days;
- weekly points for three months; and
- monthly points for one year.

Deleted data may therefore remain in encrypted backups until the relevant
retention period expires, but it is not restored except as part of disaster
recovery.

## Your choices and questions

You may edit optional profile data, disconnect Twitch, or delete your account
from account settings. Review the account-deletion policy or use the Z1Rracing
Discord contact route for help.
