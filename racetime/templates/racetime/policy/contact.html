Contact Z1RR Raceroom

Last updated August 23, 2026

Where to ask

Use the Z1Rracing Discord for support and operational questions. Choose the channel that matches the issue:

  • Discord Moderation: conduct, harassment, account restrictions, or sensitive reports;
  • Competitive Integrity: suspected cheating or an event-rules issue;
  • Tech and Restream: site, OAuth, bot, LiveSplit, race-room, or broadcast-platform problems; and
  • the relevant event team for TTP, Torneo Corto, Triforce Trials, League, or another organized format.

What to include

Include the race URL, approximate time, your display name, what you expected, and what happened. Screenshots and exact error text are useful. Send identity, integrity, or abuse evidence privately when it contains personal information.

Never send passwords, session cookies, Discord or Twitch tokens, OAuth codes, private bot credentials, or backup keys. Operators do not need those secrets to investigate a report.

Independent operation

Z1RR Raceroom is operated by Z1Rracing. This deployment is not endorsed by or jointly administered with racetime.gg. For this site's accounts, races, moderation, policies, or availability, contact Z1Rracing rather than the racetime.gg administrators.
